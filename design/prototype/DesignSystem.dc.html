How Liana's app behaves
  • Breakpoints (classes on <html>): mobile, tablet, computer, largescreen, widescreen, hugescreen, massivescreen.
  • Mobile makes everything 1.25× bigger (text 15 px, buttons 42 px) and buttons go full width.
  • Dark mode is a filter: invert(99%) hue-rotate(180deg) on the page, with images and coloured labels inverted back.

Rules for Feedback

How we apply Liana's system.

  • Admin screens (top bar, sidebar, front page, editor, results) use Liana's sizes exactly, so Feedback looks like part of LianaMailer.
  • Answer screens (survey in the email, on a phone, on the web) stay larger: text 16 px or more, tap targets at least 44 px. They are for members, not for Liana users.
  • Green text: use #2B6450 for links and small green text. #4CA74E is for fills, icons and text 18.7 px bold or larger.
  • Purple #7662DB is only for Ailu and AI features.
  • Icons: Liana uses Font Awesome 7 Pro, which needs a licence. The prototype uses simple line icons instead.
